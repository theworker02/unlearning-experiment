\section{Conclusion}
P001 demonstrates a reproducible pipeline and motivates reference separability as a
precondition for empirical unlearning claims. Its one-percent IID deletion is
assay-insensitive under the evaluated output metrics. A prospective class-conditioned pilot
identified full-class deletion with JS divergence, while the subsequent controlled-
contamination pilot rejected the proposed exact/exact-percentile equivalence margin. The
current evidence therefore supports a bounded distinguishability study, not functional,
privacy, representation, or utility equivalence. C001 remains blocked pending a revised,
prospectively locked decision protocol.
