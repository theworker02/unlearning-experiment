\section{Research Transparency}
This manuscript distinguishes pilot, exploratory, and confirmatory analyses. P001 was
completed before public registration and is therefore described as retrospective. C001 is
specified as a prospective confirmatory study and must be registered before its first
training run. Automated systems assisted with code, organization, analysis tooling, and
drafting; empirical measurements and hashes are generated by the repository, while the
human author remains responsible for scientific decisions, interpretation, originality,
citation accuracy, and submission-policy compliance.
