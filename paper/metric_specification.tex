\section{Metric Specification and Contradiction Matrix}
\subsection{Task utility}
Accuracy is reported on retained, forgotten, validation, and test partitions with the
partition identity explicit.  Balanced accuracy and macro-F1 are added when class balance
or deletion policy makes ordinary accuracy misleading.  Utility is always compared with
the exact-retraining population: retaining the original model's accuracy is not sufficient
if exact retraining induces a materially different decision function.

\subsection{Predictive and probabilistic fidelity}
For probe set $S$, prediction agreement between models $a$ and $b$ is
\begin{equation}
 A_S(a,b)=|S|^{-1}\sum_{x\in S}\mathbf{1}
 \{\arg\max_c p_a(c\mid x)=\arg\max_c p_b(c\mid x)\}.
\end{equation}
Agreement discards confidence information, so it is paired with per-example
Jensen--Shannon divergence.  Class-conditional summaries detect discrepancies hidden by
majority classes.  The study retains the distribution of example-level values and the
model-level aggregate; only the latter supplies independent training-run replication.

Distance direction also matters. A low divergence from the original model is not generally
a fidelity objective, because the original model did train on the forgotten data. The
relevant comparison is approximate-to-exact relative to exact-to-exact variation. Reporting
both agreement and divergence avoids a false sense of consistency when labels agree but
confidence changes substantially, while retaining class-conditioned summaries makes a
single aggregate less likely to be dominated by easy classes.

\subsection{Calibration fidelity}
Negative log likelihood evaluates probability assigned to the observed class, and the
Brier score measures squared probability error over all classes.  Expected calibration
error is included with binning fixed in the protocol, accompanied by reliability curves so
that one scalar does not conceal the direction of miscalibration.  These quantities are
computed separately on forgotten, retained, and independent test data.  Calibration
fidelity means proximity to the exact population, not merely a lower calibration error
than the original model.

\subsection{Forgotten and local behavior}
Forget-set accuracy is descriptive for IID deletion and is not interpreted as direct proof
of removal.  Local analysis evaluates changes among neighbors of forgotten examples in
input or frozen representation space.  Distance bands, same-class restrictions, probe
checkpoint, and tie handling are specified before outcome inspection.  The resulting
curve asks whether the intervention creates collateral behavioral disturbance that decays
with distance from the deletion request.

Local probes require particular care because the chosen neighborhood can itself determine
the apparent result. Input-space distance may be poorly aligned with visual similarity,
whereas representation-space distance depends on a checkpoint whose training data and
layer must be declared. The protocol therefore treats neighborhood construction as part of
the experimental condition. Exploratory visualizations can generate hypotheses, but they
cannot be promoted to confirmatory evidence after the radius, embedding, or examples have
been selected by inspecting the observed effect.

\subsection{Privacy fidelity}
Membership inference is evaluated relative to exact retraining under the same attacker and
data construction.  Reported quantities include attack AUROC, advantage, and calibration
where applicable.  An approximate model is not credited merely because one attack is near
chance; its privacy behavior must be considered against both exact replicas and alternative
attacks.  P001 does not contain a validated privacy result, so no privacy conclusion is
drawn from it.

\subsection{Representation fidelity}
Compatible architectures are compared on a fixed probe set using centered kernel alignment
or another symmetry-aware method.  Layer correspondence is declared in advance.  Direct
neuron-by-neuron comparison is avoided because hidden-unit permutations can preserve the
function.  Linear probes may characterize class separability but do not by themselves
establish representational equivalence.

\subsection{Robustness fidelity}
Controlled corruptions test whether approximate and exact models respond similarly away
from the clean empirical distribution.  Candidate transformations include Gaussian noise,
blur, brightness change, and occlusion with frozen severity levels.  These are corruption
tests, not adversarial-robustness claims.  Clean and corrupted outcomes are linked to the
same model run so that uncertainty is propagated at the seed level.

\subsection{Contradiction matrix}
For every condition, the processed record contains separate decisions for forget behavior,
retained utility, privacy, calibration, prediction distribution, representation, and exact
counterfactual consistency.  Each cell is \texttt{pass}, \texttt{fail},
\texttt{inconclusive}, or \texttt{not assessed}; missing evidence is never converted to a
pass.  A contradiction is counted only when the conventional criteria and counterfactual
criterion were frozen, evaluable, and integrity-valid.  The matrix preserves component
values so that later aggregation cannot hide conflicting dimensions.

The matrix is not a composite score. Its purpose is diagnostic: it reveals whether a method
that satisfies one operational objective is nevertheless outside the counterfactual
reference envelope on another observable. Any future aggregate would need a defensible
utility model, sensitivity analysis, and a demonstration that it does not conceal these
conflicts. Until then, an \texttt{inconclusive} cell is scientifically preferable to a
forced binary success label.

The next full-class method pilot does not meet those prerequisites. It therefore emits a
continuous exploratory comparison rather than a contradiction count: every original-seed
method result retains its conventional metrics, median distance to the exact ensemble, and
location within the dependent exact/exact null. No cell is converted to pass or fail, and no
method is labeled equivalent.
