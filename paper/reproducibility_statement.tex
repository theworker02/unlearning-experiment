\section{Reproducibility Statement}
Research Release \StudyRelease{} provides source code, dataset acquisition and validation
procedures, dataset and split hashes, the immutable deletion and retained manifests, all 15
exact seeds, model and method configurations, environment metadata, checkpoint and
prediction references, raw and processed metrics, quality-gate output, F002 source data and
generation code, and artifact checksums. Level A reproduction (predictions to metrics,
tables, figures, and paper values) is supported. Level B reproduction (dataset acquisition
through training and analysis) is the target of the R001 package and remains hardware- and
software-sensitive. Level C independent reimplementation has not been demonstrated.

\paragraph{Level A: analysis reproduction.}
Starting from archived predictions, a researcher can recompute metrics and seed summaries,
regenerate validated tables and F002, regenerate LaTeX commands, and rebuild the manuscript.
The release checksum manifest verifies every included payload before analysis.

\paragraph{Level B: experiment reproduction.}
Starting from Fashion-MNIST, the configuration specifies acquisition, split seed, limits,
deletion manifest, model, training schedule, original seed, exact seeds, approximate
methods, and evaluation parameters. A reproduction creates a new immutable experiment
record; it must not overwrite P001. Runtime and floating-point results may vary across
drivers and accelerators, so acceptance depends on declared tolerances and invariant
checks rather than exact wall-clock or bitwise identity.

\paragraph{Level C: independent replication.}
An independent group would implement the written protocol without relying on hidden local
state and assess whether the methodological conclusion persists. No such replication has
been completed. The manuscript consequently distinguishes reproducibility infrastructure
from independently reproduced evidence.
