# Preprint v1.1 status

This is the local R001 pilot and methodology preprint. It reports P001 pipeline and
convergence observations, its retrospective U0 assay classification, a prospective
class-conditioned assay calibration, and a prospectively frozen negative equivalence-margin
calibration. It does not report successful unlearning, a confirmatory method ranking,
cross-dataset generalization, or a certified deletion result.

The canonical sources are `manuscript.tex`, `manuscript.md`, `metadata.yaml`, and the
generated publication results. Public submission has not occurred, and no DOI or persistent
identifier has been assigned. The author is Matthew Looney. C001 remains blocked until a
revised decision protocol is frozen and prospectively registered.
