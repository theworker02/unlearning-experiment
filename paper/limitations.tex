\section{Limitations and Threats to Validity}
\subsection{External validity}
The current evidence covers one reduced Fashion-MNIST split, one small CNN, one IID
deletion manifest, and one original-model seed in P001. It does not establish behavior on
the full Fashion-MNIST training set, CIFAR, tabular data, larger architectures, sequential
requests, structured deletions, or different optimization regimes. The multi-original
and controlled-contamination assays are separate pilots rather than confirmatory evidence.
Their full-class results cover only classes 0 and 5.

\subsection{Construct validity}
The frozen convergence rule covers JS divergence and prediction disagreement only. It does
not validate calibration, privacy, representation, local, relearning, or corruption probes.
Representation and parameter similarity do not uniquely determine functional influence.
Membership attacks are incomplete adversaries whose outcomes depend on threat model,
features, and attack training data. No aggregate score has been justified.

\subsection{Statistical validity}
P001 contains 15 exact seeds but only one original seed and one deletion manifest. The
retrospective separability analysis was not preregistered and cannot supply confirmatory
error control. Pairwise exact distances are dependent. The frozen 10\% convergence
threshold is an engineering diagnostic, not a power guarantee or equivalence margin.
The class-conditioned precision simulation selected ten exact seeds for JS, but only under
the observed finite ensemble and frozen pilot rule. The controlled one-percent contamination
calibration rejected the proposed exact/exact-percentile equivalence margins. No claim is
made that 10 or 15 seeds, or any numerical margin, transfers beyond these pilot conditions.

\subsection{Implementation validity}
Fine-tuning and naive gradient ascent are simple engineering baselines. Although SCRUB is
literature-backed, P001 is not a full reproduction or competitive hyperparameter study.
Post-pilot objective auditing found an architecture-specific validity failure: the deterministic
small CNN gives the identically initialized student and teacher identical logits at SCRUB's first
max-step. The KL term therefore supplies no model-derived ascent signal at that point; numerical
residuals amplified by Adam are not accepted as evidence of the published forgetting mechanism.
The archived SCRUB output is retained as an immutable interface smoke test but is invalid as method
reproduction or competitive evidence. New runs fail closed unless the initial max-step signal is
validated.
The environment records a single AMD/ROCm/WSL2 execution; bitwise cross-hardware
reproducibility and independent laboratory replication have not been demonstrated.

\subsection{Privacy and legal scope}
Approximate unlearning does not by itself establish legal compliance or demonstrate the
absence of private information. Empirical proximity to a finite exact-retraining ensemble
is not a certificate of erasure. Fashion-MNIST is non-sensitive benchmark data; conclusions
about operational deletion requests involving personal information would require a
separate threat model, governance process, and privacy analysis.

\subsection{Measurement and selection threats}
The meaning of a fidelity result depends on the probe distribution and the deletion rule.
Aggregate test metrics can be insensitive to a localized change near forgotten examples,
while a forget-set metric can be confounded by ordinary class generalization. Likewise,
high-loss, class-conditioned, clustered, or corrupted-label selections may create a
substantially different counterfactual target than IID selection. The current work does not
claim that a single collection of metrics is sufficient. It supplies a framework for
reporting disagreement among metric families and requires future studies to preserve the
selection algorithm, its seed, and its provenance alongside the deletion IDs.

Finite probe sets also limit what can be observed. Prediction agreement and JS divergence
are computed on enumerated partitions; two models can agree on those points and differ
elsewhere. Conversely, finite exact ensembles can make an approximate output appear
unusual because the reference distribution is itself estimated imprecisely. Bootstrap,
permutation, and equivalence procedures in a confirmatory protocol must account for this
estimation problem, dependent pairwise comparisons, and the multiplicity introduced by
methods, datasets, deletion regimes, and metric families.

\subsection{Temporal scope and method readiness}
This manuscript reports the completed P001 pilot and its associated calibration material.
It is not a retrospective container for every later development run in the repository.
Mechanism-validation failures and tuning failures are retained in the failure registry so
that they cannot disappear from the scientific record, but a failed development method is
not converted into a statistical observation or a headline result. Before a literature-backed
method enters a comparative claim, its provenance, license, implementation deviations,
mechanism checks, and prospectively declared tuning policy must be reviewed.

The same restriction applies to the planned main study. A multi-original-seed assay,
stronger deletion stressors, sequential trajectories, and additional datasets are necessary
to distinguish a pilot measurement issue from a reproducible phenomenon. Until those
conditions are frozen and executed, language about evaluation contradictions remains a
research question rather than an empirical conclusion.

\subsection{Publication status}
R001 is a locally verified release candidate. At the time of writing it has no public OSF,
Zenodo, GitHub release, or preprint identifier, and the author metadata still requires
human verification. These omissions are recorded rather than filled with invented
identifiers.

Publication readiness is therefore a release gate, not a document-length judgment. It
requires verified citations, a claims--evidence graph for every substantive quantitative
statement, complete primary experiments under a frozen plan, documented failures, and a
clean-environment reproduction of at least one flagship result. The absence of a public
identifier is not a temporary formatting defect: no DOI, registration number, or archival
URL should be inserted until the corresponding external action has actually occurred.
