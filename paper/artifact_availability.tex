\section{Data and Artifact Availability}
The local R001 release bundle is machine-verifiable. No public repository release, OSF DOI,
Zenodo DOI, or preprint identifier existed at build time; those fields remain explicitly
unassigned. Fashion-MNIST is obtained from its cited source. The archived deletion manifest
is identified by its complete SHA-256 value in the machine-readable reproduction manifest
and release checksum inventory. Identifiers will be inserted only after the corresponding
public services assign them.
