\section{Related Work}
\subsection{Definitions and counterfactual targets}
Certified removal formalizes a distributional relationship between an updated learning
system and retraining \cite{guo2020certified}.  Stability-oriented work studies deletion
bounds for particular objective classes \cite{neel2021descent}, while generalization-based
formulations clarify that correct classification of a deleted IID example need not imply
instance-specific memory \cite{sekhari2021remember}.  These distinctions motivate the
present separation between procedural exclusion, empirical behavioral similarity, and a
formal certificate.  This paper supplies the first two for a pilot and claims neither that
the empirical envelope is a certificate nor that matching finite probes establishes erasure.

\subsection{Efficient unlearning methods}
SISA partitions training into shards and slices so that a deletion can trigger partial
rather than complete retraining \cite{bourtoule2021machine}.  Gradient-based approaches
instead update a trained model, with objectives that may increase loss on forgotten data,
recover utility on retained data, or combine teacher and student signals.  SCRUB is a
representative deep-network method evaluated in the unbounded-unlearning setting
\cite{kurmanji2023unbounded}.  Selective Synaptic Dampening modifies parameters according
to estimated importance \cite{foster2024ssd}.  The method classes make different structural
assumptions and expose different cost profiles; consequently, a single forget-accuracy
number is not a sufficient common target.

The P001 implementation includes fine-tuning and naive gradient ascent as simple
engineering baselines and a clean-room SCRUB implementation.  Their presence validates a
common interface but does not establish competitive reproduction.  A method enters the
confirmatory comparison only after its paper, implementation provenance, license,
deviations, and at least one sanity-check behavior are recorded.

\subsection{Privacy attacks and behavioral evidence}
Membership inference tests whether an adversary can distinguish members from non-members
using model behavior \cite{shokri2017membership}.  It is an important privacy diagnostic,
but an attack result is conditional on the adversary, features, reference population, and
training data.  Chance-level performance for one attack does not demonstrate that the
unlearned model behaves like an exact retrain, just as prediction agreement does not rule
out privacy leakage.  The proposed contradiction matrix therefore reports privacy beside,
rather than as a substitute for, predictive, probabilistic, calibration, representation,
and local endpoints.

\subsection{Auditability and benchmark methodology}
Auditable definitions motivate retaining the executed algorithm, deletion request, data
lineage, and exclusion evidence rather than attempting to infer the entire procedure from
outputs \cite{thudi2022auditable}.  Our artifact design follows this principle by coupling
behavioral records with immutable manifests and checkpoint hashes.  Deep-unlearning
benchmarks compare utility and forgetting objectives \cite{kurmanji2023unbounded}; the
specific measurement question here is whether those surface criteria can disagree with a
population of exact counterfactual models.  The contribution sought is therefore an
evaluation design and empirical contradiction study, not another isolated method.

Wichert and Sikdar show directly that random forget sets can make an unlearning method
difficult to distinguish from retaining the original model, and recommend influential
instances for more diagnostic evaluation \cite{wichert2024rethinking}.  MUSE broadens
evaluation to six properties and repeated deletion requests for language models
\cite{shi2025muse}.  Gao et al. demonstrate that favorable output behavior may coexist
with recoverable forgotten information in internal representations
\cite{gao2026illusion}.  These results substantially narrow the present contribution:
neither metric disagreement, representation auditing, sequential evaluation, nor the
weakness of random deletion is claimed as new.  Lanyon et al. further show that evaluation
can depend materially on the original model training seed and that additional unlearning
seeds do not generally compensate for a single-training-seed design
\cite{lanyon2026seeds}.  Accordingly, varying original training seeds is a required control,
not a contribution claimed by this study.  The unresolved question is whether a
prespecified assay can calibrate approximate-model discrepancies against the natural
distribution of independently exact-retrained models and identify contradictions without
using approximate-method outcomes to choose the test.

\subsection{Scope of the present evidence}
Fashion-MNIST supplies a modest neural test bed \cite{xiao2017fashion}.  It is useful for
exercising stochastic training and probability-level metrics but cannot establish
cross-dataset or architecture-level generality.  Digits is reserved for deterministic CI.
The staged program moves to stronger vision and tabular settings only after the reference
assay and statistical rules are frozen.  The complete literature-review and citation-audit
records are maintained separately so that unverified recollection cannot silently enter
the bibliography.
