\section{Reproducibility Appendix}
P001 is identified by `fashion-cnn-random-01pct-schema2-rocm-pilot-v1`; its exact seeds are
2001--2015 and its deletion manifest is archived under `experiments/P001`. Original
training took \PilotOriginalSeconds{} seconds and all exact retrainings took
\PilotExactSeconds{} seconds on the recorded RX 7800 XT/ROCm environment. These device
measurements are not general performance estimates. The repository stores complete
configurations, failures, environment metadata, prediction/checkpoint hashes, and figure
source provenance.

\subsection{Frozen identifiers}
The original training seed is 11, split seed is 1729, and deletion seed is 1009. Exact
training uses seeds 2001 through 2015. The deletion manifest contains 90 IDs and the
retained complement 8,910 IDs. Their full SHA-256 values are stored in
\texttt{reproduction.yaml} and the R001 checksum inventory rather than manually duplicated
throughout the prose.

\subsection{Optimization details}
Original and exact training use AdamW for four epochs with batch size 128, learning rate
$10^{-3}$, and weight decay $10^{-4}$. The unlearning interface uses Adam, learning rate
$2\times10^{-4}$, one epoch, retain batch size 128, and forget batch size 64. The raw
configuration remains authoritative for method-specific alpha, gamma, loss weights,
temperature, step limits, and any future correction.

\subsection{Quality-gate semantics}
A PASS means the corresponding property was checked for the archived execution. It does
not mean the model successfully unlearned. Publication eligibility is separately encoded
and is false for P001. Failed experiments are metadata but are not included as statistical
observations.

\subsection{Paper generation}
The build reads the immutable raw result and processed convergence and separability records,
writes \texttt{results/publication/paper-results.json}, generates tables and LaTeX commands,
validates citations and claim links, builds the manuscript and supplement, and records
hashes in \texttt{paper/provenance.json}. The R001 release builder copies these outputs,
creates a source archive, computes SHA-256 for each payload, and refuses to overwrite an
existing release directory.

\subsection{Prospective boundary}
C001 is not authorized to run. Two local calibration pilots were completed before C001:
the 50-model class-conditioned assay and the 95-model controlled-contamination margin test.
Both remain pilot evidence. Before the first confirmatory training job, a public prospective
registration must freeze its hypotheses, experimental units, datasets, deletion regimes,
manifest generation, training and exact seed counts, primary and secondary metrics,
distinguishability rules, correction family, exclusions, failure policy, and stopping rule.
Any equivalence claim requires a new admissible margin. Changes after registration must be
timestamped and labeled exploratory or confirmatory-impacting.

\subsection{Prospective margin-calibration artifacts}
The failed v1 calibration, the unexecuted global-selection v2 draft, and the class-stratified
v2.1 alternative are retained as distinct versioned objects.  The v2.1 package contains a
machine-readable protocol, a checksummed input plan, exact calibration and holdout seed lists,
hashes for 30 reusable checkpoints, planned identifiers for 20 new exact trainings, a guarded
validation-prediction exporter, and a checkpoint preflight report.  The preflight verifies
checkpoint hashes, finite tensor state, strict SmallCNN schema compatibility, and the frozen
1,000-example validation split; it does not access test predictions or train models.  The human
SESOI review record remains null and therefore blocks the freeze.

The \VTwoOneNewTrainingJobs{} planned new exact trainings also have a hardware-specific planning
estimate generated from the 30 condition-matched pilot runtimes: a serial median of
\VTwoOneTrainingMedianSeconds{} seconds with a 95\% bootstrap interval of
\VTwoOneTrainingLowSeconds{}--\VTwoOneTrainingHighSeconds{} seconds.  This is not a fresh
benchmark and excludes validation inference, startup, scheduling, memory, and transfer to any
other environment; it therefore does not estimate total calibration runtime.
