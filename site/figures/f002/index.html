F002 — Exact-retraining seed convergence

Question

Do exact/exact distance estimates stabilize as independently retrained replicas are added in this pilot condition?

Axes and unit

x is the ordered prefix population size k. y is the mean exact/exact test-set distance. The unit is a pair of independently trained models; uncertainty uses replica bootstrap resampling.

Allowed interpretation

The frozen k=10→15 diagnostic passed for JS divergence and prediction disagreement in this condition.

Disallowed interpretation

This does not prove k=15 is adequate elsewhere, establish equivalence, or demonstrate successful unlearning.
