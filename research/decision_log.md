# Scientific decision log

## 2026-08-30 — Counterfactual target

Exact retraining is represented by a distribution over independent seeds, not one model,
because stochastic training produces natural behavioral variation.

## 2026-08-30 — Digits scope

Digits is restricted to infrastructure validation. It cannot support headline or publication
claims because its scale and model regime do not address the intended generalization question.

## 2026-08-30 — Pilot maturity

The Fashion-MNIST subset experiments remain `PILOT`. Their descriptive envelope diagnostics
may inform design but cannot be retroactively treated as confirmatory evidence.

## 2026-08-30 — ROCm environment

AMD ROCm on the RX 7800 XT is a compute backend, not a scientific scope restriction. CPU and
accelerator runs use the same experiment interfaces and are separate environment strata.

## 2026-08-30 — Phase III fail-closed policy

Legacy pilot runs lack raw prediction artifacts and complete trajectories. They remain intact
and are expected to fail publication postconditions rather than being edited or promoted.
