# Tier-0/Tier-1 infrastructure validation — not publication evidence.

Digits runs validate machinery only. The Fashion-MNIST counterfactual-fidelity pilot is
development evidence and must not be pooled with the future confirmatory study. Neither
may support headline or novelty claims. The final scope remains
multiple publication datasets, architectures, validated unlearning methods, deletion
regimes, sequential requests, exact-retraining distributions, and preregistered statistics.
