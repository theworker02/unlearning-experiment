# NR001 — High accuracy on IID-deleted examples

P001 approximate-method forget accuracies ranged from 0.900 to 0.922. This is a retained
observation, not evidence that the exact samples remained memorized: an independently
retrained classifier can recognize ordinary Fashion-MNIST categories without those
particular training images. The exact-retraining reference—not low absolute accuracy—is
the counterfactual target. Retrospective separability analysis classified the 1% IID assay
as U0, so P001 supports neither successful- nor failed-unlearning claims.
